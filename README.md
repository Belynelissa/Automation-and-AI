# Commercial Intelligence | Sales Analytics, Customer Segmentation & Churn

## Engagement context

This analytical work was presented to paying clients. The public repository contains sample datasets and analytical materials rather than confidential client records. It demonstrates how business data can be explored to support commercial, customer and operational decisions while protecting client information.

## What this work addresses

Businesses generate data across sales activity, customer relationships and service interactions. The value comes from connecting that information to practical questions: where sales are coming from, how customers differ, and which patterns may signal retention or growth opportunities.

This repository brings together two related areas of commercial analytics.

## 1. Sales performance and commercial analysis

The sales analysis explores sales data to help examine performance patterns and communicate findings through visual outputs and a supporting report.

**Business questions include:**
- How is sales performance distributed across the available dimensions?
- Which categories or segments contribute most to recorded sales?
- What patterns should management investigate when reviewing commercial performance?

**Decision support:** provide a clearer basis for sales reviews, performance discussions and identification of areas for further investigation.

## 2. Customer behaviour, segmentation and churn

The customer analytics work examines churn patterns and customer groupings, supported by visual analysis and feature investigation.

**Business questions include:**
- Which customer characteristics are associated with churn in the sample?
- How do service or contract attributes relate to customer outcomes?
- Can customer clustering reveal groups with different behavioural profiles?
- Which variables appear influential in the analytical work and merit further review?

**Decision support:** help retention and customer teams investigate customer segments, service experiences and potential retention priorities.

## Analytical work and evidence

The repository includes:
- Sales analysis and a supporting report.
- Churn analysis using a telecommunications customer dataset.
- Customer clustering outputs.
- Feature correlation and feature-importance visualisations.
- Jupyter notebooks documenting the analytical workflow.

The notebooks and saved visualisations provide the supporting evidence. No model accuracy, revenue uplift, churn reduction or other measured business outcome is claimed here unless it is explicitly reported and validated in the underlying analysis.

## Business value

Together, these analyses demonstrate how data can support better commercial conversations by helping teams:

- Review sales patterns and identify areas that deserve management attention.
- Understand differences between customer groups.
- Investigate factors associated with churn.
- Explore customer segments for more relevant engagement.
- Use analytical evidence to frame practical follow-up questions.

The work provides decision support; realised financial or operational impact depends on the quality of the underlying data and the actions taken by the business.

## Data and interpretation

- Public examples use sample datasets and do not publish confidential client records.
- The telecommunications churn dataset is a sample dataset, not a client's live customer base.
- Relationships and feature importance do not establish causation.
- Customer clusters are analytical groupings and should be interpreted in context before operational use.
- Predictive outputs require suitable validation and ongoing monitoring before they are used to guide customer-level decisions.
